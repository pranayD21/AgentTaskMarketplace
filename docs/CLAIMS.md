# HANDOFF benchmark claims

HANDOFF's official benchmark results are **30.8% faster task completion** and **53.2% cheaper inference**. The project team reports that these results were tested by an independent third party. The underlying report and measurements are covered by an NDA and are not published in this repository.

These are the project's official performance figures. The [claim record](benchmark-claims.jsonl) preserves their exact values, units, provenance, and disclosure status for documentation and demo use. The older [bounded-v1 protocol](buyer/benchmark-plan.md) and its saved first-party run artifacts are a separate evaluation; do not substitute those runs for the NDA-covered third-party benchmark.

Use the same wording everywhere: **“HANDOFF completed tasks 30.8% faster with 53.2% cheaper inference in independent third-party testing.”** Do not round these official values or relabel the inference comparison as total system cost.
